ROLL 36 is an analog film camera with one rule: a roll has 36 frames, and you cannot look at any of them until the roll is developed.

SHOOT A REAL ROLL
Load a roll, pick a film stock, and shoot. The counter only goes down. When it hits zero, the camera locks. Your photos stay hidden while you shoot, so every press of the shutter counts. No preview, no delete, no retake.

DEVELOP AND WAIT
When the roll is done, send it to the darkroom. Developing takes a real 60 minutes, and it keeps running even if you close the app. There is no skip button and nothing to buy to speed it up. When the timer ends, all 36 frames appear at once on a contact sheet. View them full screen, share them, or save them to Photos.

IMPORT TO THE DARKROOM
Already have photos? Choose any photo from your library, pick a film stock, preview the look, and develop it. Imported photos do not use up your roll.

14 FILM STOCKS, FOUR GROUPS
Each stock has its own colour, contrast, grain and halation.
• Portrait: Portra 400, Ektar 100, Pro 400H, Gold 200, UltraMax 400
• Black and white: Tri-X 400, HP5 Plus, Acros 100
• Night: Cinestill 800T, Portra 800
• Expired and slide: Expired Gold, Expired Fuji, Velvia 50, Provia 100F

PRICING, STATED PLAINLY
ROLL 36 is free to download, with Portra 400 included free. The other 13 film stocks are one-time in-app purchases, sold individually or as one full set. There is no subscription, no account and no ads.
Free version: Portra 400, 5 imported photos, one roll developing at a time, and a small ROLL 36 mark on exported photos. Any purchase removes the mark and the limits on imports and parallel developing. Restore Purchases is in Settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no analytics. Your photos stay on your device and are not uploaded anywhere. Because there is no cloud, deleting the app also deletes your photos, so save the ones you want to keep to Photos first.

Available in English and Vietnamese.

Film stock names are used only to describe the look each stock is inspired by. ROLL 36 is not affiliated with or endorsed by any film manufacturer.
