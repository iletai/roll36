No login, account or demo credentials are required. The app works fully offline and has no backend.

WHAT THE APP DOES
ROLL 36 is an analog film camera. The user loads a roll of 36 frames and shoots. Frames are intentionally hidden until the roll is developed, as on real film. After shooting, the user starts developing and waits; when the wait ends, all frames appear on a contact sheet. A darkroom tab also applies film looks to photos the user imports from the library.

PERMISSIONS
- Camera: used only to take photos inside the app (the viewfinder). If denied, the app shows a screen explaining how to enable it in Settings.
- Photos: photos are imported with the system PhotosPicker (no full-library access) and saved to the library only when the user taps Save.
No data is collected, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

DESIGN FEATURES THAT MAY LOOK LIKE BUGS
1. Captured frames cannot be viewed before developing. This is the core mechanic of the app, not a missing feature. The viewfinder only shows the frame number, not the photo.
2. Developing takes 60 minutes of real time, by design. It continues if the app is closed. There is deliberately no skip or "speed up" option.
   To review a developed roll quickly: on the real device the full flow needs the 60-minute wait. Imported photos in the Darkroom tab are developed immediately (no wait), so all film looks and purchases can be reviewed without waiting.

HOW TO TEST THE IN-APP PURCHASES (sandbox)
Free: Portra 400, 5 imported photos, one roll developing at a time, small "ROLL 36" mark on exported photos.
1. Open the Library tab. Tap any locked film stock, or "Unlock all 14 stocks", to open the purchase sheet.
2. Buy either the full set (non-consumable, product id <bundle id>.full) or a single stock (non-consumable, <bundle id>.film.<stockID>, for example .film.trix400).
3. After a purchase the stock shows "Owned" and the mark is removed from exported photos.
4. Restore Purchases is available on the Library screen and in Settings.
All products are non-consumable, one-time purchases. There are no subscriptions and no consumables.
